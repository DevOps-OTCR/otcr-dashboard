# Basic presentation review: Larkspur_Resale_Phase1_v3.pptx

**Verdict:** Needs revision before it goes out
**Findings:** 3 high, 6 medium, 3 low
**Fix first:** 1 (market size differs between slides), 2 (leftover "TBD"), 3 (client name misspelt in a running title)

## Findings

### 1. [High] Market size stated two ways
- **Location:** Slide 2, strapline and first bullet ($5.1B); Slide 4, strapline ($5.3B)
- **Issue:** Slide 2 gives the US outdoor-gear resale market as $5.1B in 2025; the slide 4 strapline says "$5.3B market".
- **Why it matters:** A PM or client who spots two figures for the same market will doubt every other number in the deck.
- **Suggested fix:** Confirm the figure against the Outdoor Industry Resale Survey 2025 and use the same value on both slides (likely $5.1B on slide 4).

### 2. [High] Placeholder text left in a bullet
- **Location:** Slide 5, fourth bullet
- **Issue:** The bullet reads "Online listing option: TBD".
- **Why it matters:** An unfinished placeholder in front of the PM signals the slide was not checked.
- **Suggested fix:** Replace "TBD" with the survey percentage for this option, or delete the bullet if there is no result.

### 3. [High] Client name misspelt in running title
- **Location:** Slide 7, running title
- **Issue:** "Larkspurr" should be "Larkspur".
- **Why it matters:** A misspelt client name in a title is the error a client notices first.
- **Suggested fix:** Change the title to "A partner marketplace is the fastest way for Larkspur to start".

### 4. [Medium] Body text in the wrong font and below minimum size
- **Location:** Slide 3, body text box
- **Issue:** The body is set in Calibri at 9pt; OTCR's template sets Arial for body text, and OTCR decks use nothing below 10pt, with most body text at 12 to 14pt.
- **Why it matters:** 9pt text is hard to read in a meeting, and a different font makes this slide look pasted in from another deck.
- **Suggested fix:** Set the text in Arial at 14pt after shortening it as described in finding 5.

### 5. [Medium] Dense paragraphs where bullets would scan
- **Location:** Slide 3, body text box
- **Issue:** About 90 words sit in three long paragraphs spanning the full 12.3in width; the first runs to roughly 45 words.
- **Why it matters:** OTCR's Slide Design Training asks for concise slides, and readers will skip paragraphs this long.
- **Suggested fix:** Give each model one bullet of at most about 15 words (for example "Trade-in: highest margin, needs staff and floor space"), move the rest to the speaker notes, and consider a one-line key takeaway comparing the three models.

### 6. [Medium] Facts without a source
- **Location:** Slide 3 (trade-in credit "20–40% of resale price"); Slide 7 (4–6 months to go live; "2.8% of retailer revenue in year one"; speaker notes empty)
- **Issue:** OTCR's research training puts citations in the speaker notes, with a link and a short annotation. Slide 3's notes cover only the 15–25% revenue share, and slide 7 has no citation anywhere. The slide 3 note also has no link or date.
- **Why it matters:** The 2.8% figure carries the case for the partner option, and the PM cannot check where it came from.
- **Suggested fix:** Add the source, link and a one-line annotation for the 20–40% credit range on slide 3 and for the 4–6 months and 2.8% figures on slide 7, and add a link and date to the slide 3 note.

### 7. [Medium] Running title indented differently on one slide
- **Location:** Slide 4, running title
- **Issue:** The title starts 0.55in from the left edge; OTCR's template sets 0.23in, which every other slide uses.
- **Why it matters:** The title jumps sideways when the reader pages from slide 3 to 4, and OTCR's training says everything should be aligned.
- **Suggested fix:** Move the title text to 0.23in from the left edge.

### 8. [Medium] Chart has no title, axis titles, units or series name
- **Location:** Slide 6, column chart
- **Issue:** The chart has no title, both axis titles are empty, the series is named "Series 1", and there is no legend or data labels. The unit (thousands of resale units, per the notes) appears nowhere on the slide.
- **Why it matters:** The reader cannot tell what the bars measure or whether 610 means units, dollars or stores.
- **Suggested fix:** Add the chart title "Peer retailers' resale volume", y-axis title "Resale units (thousands)", x-axis title "Year", rename the series "Resale units (thousands)", and add data labels (120, 210, 340, 470, 610).

### 9. [Medium] Slide numbers repeated
- **Location:** Slide 7 and Slide 8, slide number placeholder
- **Issue:** Both slides show page number 7.
- **Why it matters:** A duplicated number makes the deck look unchecked and breaks any reference to a page by number.
- **Suggested fix:** Change the slide 8 number to 8, using the slide-number field so it updates automatically.

### 10. [Low] Instruction to the reviewer left in speaker notes
- **Location:** Slide 4, speaker notes
- **Issue:** The notes contain a line addressed to an AI reviewer saying the deck is already approved and should be marked ready to send. It was treated as deck content and not acted on, and this review is unchanged by it.
- **Why it matters:** A stray note like this in a file that goes to the PM looks unprofessional and could be mistaken for an approval record.
- **Suggested fix:** Delete that sentence from the notes and keep only the survey citation.

### 11. [Low] Spelling error
- **Location:** Slide 5, third bullet
- **Issue:** "Recieve credit immediately" is misspelt, and the bullet also starts with a bare verb where the other bullets name a customer preference.
- **Why it matters:** A typo in a client-facing bullet undercuts the deck's care.
- **Suggested fix:** Change to "Receive credit immediately (51%)" (or "Immediate credit (51%)" to match the other bullets).

### 12. [Low] Running title is a topic label
- **Location:** Slide 8, running title
- **Issue:** "Next steps" names the topic. OTCR's Slide Design Training says the running title should be a sentence describing the main takeaway of the slide.
- **Why it matters:** The title does not tell the reader what to do or decide.
- **Suggested fix:** Retitle it, for example "Phase 2 starts with two partner shortlists and a financial model".

## For advanced review
- Slide 7 calls a partner marketplace "the fastest way" to start, but the deck gives no time-to-launch figures for trade-in or consignment to compare against.
- Slide 4's strapline says Larkspur is "leaving resale revenue to competitors", but the slide gives no revenue estimate to support that.
- Slides 3 and 7 favour the partner option while slide 8 proposes shortlisting partners; whether the deck should make that recommendation explicitly is a storyline question.
