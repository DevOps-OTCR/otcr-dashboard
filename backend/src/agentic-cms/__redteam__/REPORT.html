DevOps-OTCR/otcr-dashboard · branch feat/agentic-cms-chat-endpoint · commit da2c1df1 · 2026-11-09 · 8 attack angles, every finding re-run by an independent skeptic, plus a completeness critic. 42 confirmed / 9 refuted, deduplicated below.

Verdict: the retry loop is solid, the parser and counter are not

The existing suite still passes, 160/160. The cut-and-retry loop does what the PR describes: it keeps the longest attempt that fits, keeps the shortest one if every attempt is over, buffers the cut so the reader never loses the draft, and saves no internal prompts. The weak spots are earlier in the chain. requestedWordLimit() misses or misreads common phrasings, and countDraftWords() can skip most of a draft. In those cases the loop never runs, or it cuts text it should have left alone. Two of Isaiah's three required cases (sectioned paper, strict-cap revision) get no check at all.

Checked and held up

  • Retry selection: keeps the longest attempt within the limit, otherwise the shortest over-limit one plus a visible notice. The cut is buffered, and the internal correction prompts are never saved.
  • Tool calls during a cut are disabled (tools: []). The flag is rechecked before every cut.
  • Thrown errors are logged and the original draft is kept.
  • Refuted by skeptics: the too-short result being shipped, the 95% vs 85% target conflict, the server instruction being spoofable as a user message, and the frontend text_replace handling.
