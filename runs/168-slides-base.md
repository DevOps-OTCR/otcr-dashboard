**Deck: "Fueling the Quad — A Wholesale Pilot for Harbor Lane Coffee"** (6 slides)

**Slide 1 — Title**
- *Harbor Lane Coffee: A Campus Wholesale Pilot*
- Subhead: 4 UIUC cafés. 12 weeks. One clear decision.

**Slide 2 — The Opportunity**
- 14 cafés across UIUC campus.
- Only 3 currently buy from a Chicago roaster — 11 are unserved or self-supplied.
- Each café uses ~40 lb/week.
- Takeaway: a large, under-penetrated local market sits in walking distance.

**Slide 3 — Why Harbor Lane Wins**
- Price: Harbor Lane $11/lb vs. competitor $12.50/lb — ~12% savings.
- Local: no Chicago freight, faster delivery, fresher roast.
- Capacity: 600 lb/week spare — a 4-café pilot needs only ~160 lb/week.
- Takeaway: cheaper, fresher, and we have room to serve it.

**Slide 4 — The Pilot**
- Scope: 4 campus cafés, 12 weeks.
- Volume: ~160 lb/week (~1,920 lb total).
- Terms: $11/lb, weekly delivery, 2-week trial ramp.
- Takeaway: low-risk, low-commitment, quickly measurable.

**Slide 5 — What Success Looks Like**
- Usage: ≥90% of pilot cafés reorder by week 6.
- Economics: pilot adds ~$1,760/week revenue.
- Proof point: becomes the reference for the remaining 10 cafés.
- Takeaway: this pilot is the wedge into all 14.

**Slide 6 — The Ask**
- Approve a 12-week, 4-café pilot at $11/lb.
- Assign an owner and start date.
- Review at week 12; scale to remaining cafés if metrics hold.

**Speaker note:** Competitor's $12.50/lb leaves room to win on both price and proximity — and 600 lb/week capacity means we can scale without new equipment.
