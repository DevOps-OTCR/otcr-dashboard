**Research Plan — UIUC Campus Wholesale Sizing (Week 3)**

*Tables to find (in order):*
1. **Dining Services contract & purchase records** — UIUC Dining procurement (FOIA-able); vendor list, coffee SKUs, annual spend.
2. **Dining outlet roster** — locations, meal counts, hours (dining.illinois.edu); drives volume-per-outlet estimates.
3. **Independent café census** — Champaign-Urbana business registry + Google/Yelp sweep; ~15–25 targets near campus.
4. **Client price list & capacity file** (already held) — anchor for conversion and feasibility.
5. **Comparable benchmarks** — NRA/SCA per-cup usage (~1.5–2 lb per 100 cups) to fill gaps.

*Verify:*
- Dining buys centrally vs. per-unit — determines one deal or many.
- Exclusivity clauses in existing contracts (blocks entry).
- Cup volumes → lb conversion assumptions; state them explicitly.
- Independent cafés' current roasters and contract end dates.
- Client capacity ceiling vs. 2-year projected demand.

**Brief**

**Objective:** Size weekly lb/week opportunity across UIUC Dining + independent campus cafés; 2-year horizon.

**Method:** Bottom-up. Dining: outlets × daily cups × 7 ÷ 100 × 1.75 lb. Independents: cafés × avg daily cups × 7, same conversion.

**Outputs:** TAM (all campus), SAM (reachable given capacity), SOM (2-yr realistic), plus a target list ranked by volume and contract timing.

**Key risks:** Centralized Dining procurement = long sales cycle; capacity caps SAM; conversion assumption ±20%.

**Ask:** Confirm capacity ceiling and any existing campus relationships before finalizing SOM.
