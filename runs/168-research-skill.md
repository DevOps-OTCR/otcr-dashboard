# How large is the wholesale opportunity across UIUC campus cafés over the next 2 years, and what evidence would a PM need before recommending it? — Research Brief

Project/workstream: Harbor Lane wholesale expansion · Prepared for: PM / LC
Decision this informs: whether Harbor Lane commits sales capacity to a wholesale pilot with campus cafés in the next two academic years.

## Executive takeaway
The opportunity is real but modest and unquantified at the level the decision needs. A defensible campus-wide revenue figure requires café counts, cups sold, and bean cost per cup — none of which we hold. The PM should treat "go" as conditional on three pieces of primary evidence: a census of campus café outlets, 3–5 buyer interviews on current supplier and price, and the café's bean cost per drink. Until then the answer is **not yet**.

## Findings
F1. The number of UIUC campus café outlets is the denominator for every sizing attempt and is not established here. [DATA NEEDED: count of campus-area cafés and coffee-serving outlets — sets the entire TAM denominator — UIUC facilities/dining directories + a walk census]
F2. Wholesale revenue is a function of outlets × cups/day × bean cost per cup × Harbor Lane's share. Harbor Lane's sellable capacity against that demand is the binding constraint the PM must price. [DATA NEEDED: Harbor Lane monthly roast capacity net of retail — decides whether demand or supply caps the pilot — internal production records]
F3. Demand-side evidence (café willingness to switch supplier) cannot be inferred from market structure; it requires direct buyer contact. [DATA NEEDED: current bean supplier, price per lb, and contract terms for 3–5 campus cafés — the only evidence that moves the switch probability — buyer interviews]

## Evidence ledger

| ID | Claim | Type | Source | Data date | Confidence |

| I1 | Campus-wide wholesale opportunity is not sizeable enough to commit capacity without primary validation. | INFERENCE | F1–F3 | — | Low |

## Limitations
The weakest link is F1: without an outlet count, no revenue range can be stated. Every downstream number is blocked on this.

## Open questions
1. How many coffee-serving outlets exist on and adjacent to campus? — sets TAM.
2. What do campus cafés currently pay per pound, and to whom? — sets price ceiling.
3. What is Harbor Lane's net roast capacity? — sets the ceiling on share.
