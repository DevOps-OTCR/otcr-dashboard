**Research Brief: Harbor Lane Coffee — UIUC Campus Café Wholesale Opportunity**

**Bottom line:** Estimated $420K–$650K annual wholesale bean opportunity across UIUC campus cafés, growing to $500K–$780K by 2026. Evidence is directionally favorable but insufficient for a PM recommendation without primary validation.

**Market sizing (top-down + bottom-up triangulation)**
- UIUC campus + Campustown: ~50–60 cafés, dining halls, and coffee-serving outlets (UIUC Dining, Illini Union, coffee shops along Green St.).
- Assume 35–45 qualified buyers (daily espresso/drip operations); ~25–30 currently buy wholesale from regional roasters (Intelligentsia, Dark Matter, Columbia Street).
- Average campus café consumes 300–500 lb/month green-equivalent; blended wholesale price $9–$13/lb.
- Bottom-up: 40 buyers × 400 lb/mo × $11 × 12 = ~$2.1M total category. Harbor Lane realistic capture 20–30% = **$420K–$650K**.

**Two-year outlook:** UIUC enrollment growth (+2–3%/yr) and campus dining expansion support 15–20% category growth. Realistic serviceable opportunity rises to $500K–$780K by 2026, assuming no major competitor lock-in.

**Evidence a PM needs before recommending**
1. **Demand validation:** 10–15 café owner interviews confirming willingness to switch/dual-source and acceptable price points.
2. **Competitive audit:** Current roaster contracts, terms, and switching costs (equipment, training, exclusivity).
3. **Unit economics:** COGS, delivery/logistics for campus density, gross margin target >40%.
4. **Capacity check:** Roasting capacity headroom to add 15K–25K lb/yr without disrupting retail.
5. **Pilot data:** 1–2 café pilot over 8–12 weeks measuring reorder rate and retention.

**Risks:** Long incumbent contracts; low switching incentive if quality parity; thin margins on small-drop deliveries; UIUC procurement rules for campus-owned outlets.

**Recommendation:** Proceed to validation, not commitment. Run 3 discovery interviews + one paid pilot before any go decision. Confidence: medium.

*Sources: UIUC Dining Services, Campustown business listings, specialty coffee wholesale benchmarks. Figures are estimates pending primary research.*
